Assumptions & method

A local coastal digital-twin prototype combining reality capture, mapped port assets, and current weather to explore higher-water scenarios and illustrate asset exposure.

Scenarios

Illustrative what-if water scenarios, not a calibrated tidal or flood forecast. Sky, cloud and rain effects are scenario illustrations: they are not driven by the weather panel, and the weather feed does not set the water level or the analysis.

Assessed inventory

Buildings and other assets visible only in the reality capture (Gaussian splat) are not discrete inventory records and are not included.

Assumed replacement values (USD)

    Every BIM model uses the same assumed value, irrespective of size or use. Amounts cover the assessed inventory only — this is not a depth–damage, repair-cost or economic-loss calculation. If a dataset, elevation or valuation is missing, the total is withheld rather than shown as complete.

    Method

    Each asset's ground elevation is sampled from the scene's terrain elevation service and compared with a flat what-if water surface; assets at or below the surface count as exposed. Each BIM model is tested at one representative point (the center of its extent), not a footprint or floor level. Flow paths, drainage, seawall overtopping, waves, velocity and duration are not modeled, and the visual detail of the reality capture does not make this comparison more precise.

    Land-sample estimate

    Share of a 60 × 60 grid of terrain samples within the reality-mesh extent that lies above the Normal reference surface but at or below the scenario surface. A sampling estimate, not an inundation boundary; samples without elevation data are excluded.

    Marker colors

    Modeled water depth at the asset: orange below 1.5 m, red at or above 1.5 m. Not a damage category or probability.
